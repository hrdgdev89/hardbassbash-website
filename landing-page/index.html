Who We Are

RESHAPING
THE NIGHT

HardBassBash is Jakarta's premier hard techno collective, born from a deep passion for raw, uncompromising sound. We exist to push the boundaries of Indonesia's underground electronic music scene through high-impact events, artist development, and community building.

From the sweat-soaked floors of Jakarta to the intimate spaces of Bandung, the cultural pulse of Yogyakarta, and the coastal energy of Surabaya — we bring hard techno to every corner of Indonesia with no compromises.
